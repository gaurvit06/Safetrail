## Numbers on slide 6

From `docs/BENCHMARK.md` (100 simulated trips, four setups, Google's token counts at list price): 82% fewer tokens per trip than the naive way; 2.9 to 3.1 paise per monitored trip; 72% of repeat-day calls served from Prune's cache; counts 100% correct. Do not quote Prune's own dollar figures (they leave out output tokens), and don't claim routing savings (Prune rerouted no calls).
